Nawa Utama is a publisher, bookstore, and book catalog website developed to showcase and manage published books through a structured digital platform.

## Overview

A professional and structured publishing website designed to present book collections, publisher information, author profiles, and other publishing-related content in one accessible platform.

The website features a custom-built catalog system developed from scratch through a custom WordPress plugin, tailored to the specific requirements of Nawa Utama's book catalog management and presentation.

Built with WordPress and Elementor, the website is optimized for search engine visibility and loading performance, providing a fast and accessible browsing experience for readers, authors, and visitors.

## Stack

* WordPress
* Custom WordPress Plugin
* Elementor
* SEO Optimization
* Speed & Performance Optimization
* Cloud Hosting
